ABOUT UIDD

Pioneering the future of digital experiences through innovative design and cutting-edge technology

OUR STORY

Founded in 2018, UIDD emerged from a shared vision to bridge the gap between exceptional design and robust technology. Our journey began in Colombo, Sri Lanka, with a small team of passionate designers and developers determined to create digital experiences that truly make a difference.

Over the years, we've grown into a multidisciplinary agency, but our core philosophy remains unchanged: to deliver solutions that not only meet business objectives but also create meaningful connections between brands and their audiences.

Today, we're proud to have collaborated with startups, enterprises, and everything in between, helping them transform their digital presence and achieve remarkable results.

OUR JOURNEY

2018 - Foundation

Establishing Our Vision

UIDD was founded with a mission to create digital experiences that blend innovative design with cutting-edge technology. Our first office opened in Colombo with a team of 5 passionate individuals.

2019 - Growth

Expanding Our Expertise

We expanded our service offerings to include full-stack development and branding. Our team grew to 10 members, and we completed our first major enterprise project.

2020 - Adaptation

Thriving in Challenge

Despite global challenges, we adapted to remote work and continued delivering exceptional results. We launched our first proprietary design system and saw a 40% growth in client base.

2021 - Innovation

Pushing Boundaries

We introduced advanced technologies like AI integration and progressive web apps to our service offerings. Our team expanded to 15 members with specialized expertise.

2022 - Recognition

Industry Acknowledgement

Received multiple awards for design excellence and innovation. Expanded our international client portfolio with projects across Asia, Europe, and North America.

2023 - Present

Leading the Future

Continuing to push the boundaries of digital innovation with emerging technologies like AR/VR integration, blockchain solutions, and advanced data visualization.
