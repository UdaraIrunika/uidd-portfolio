Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A simple website might take 4-6 weeks, while a complex web application could take 3-6 months. We'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.

What is your development process?

We follow a four-phase process: Discovery (research and planning), Design (wireframes and visuals), Development (building the solution), and Launch (testing and deployment). We maintain open communication throughout.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Yes! We offer maintenance packages and ongoing support to ensure your project continues to perform optimally. We can also help with updates, feature additions, and scaling as your business grows.

What technologies do you work with?

We specialize in modern web technologies including React, Vue.js, Next.js, Node.js, and more. For mobile, we work with React Native and Flutter. We always choose the best technology stack for your specific needs.

Can you help with an existing project?

Absolutely! We can take over existing projects, perform code audits, implement new features, or help with redesigns. We're experienced in working with various codebases and technologies.
