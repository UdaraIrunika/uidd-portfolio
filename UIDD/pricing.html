Pricing FAQs

Common questions about our pricing

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade to a higher plan at any time. We'll calculate the difference and apply it to your invoice.

Do you offer discounts for long-term projects?

Absolutely! We offer special discounts for ongoing projects and maintenance contracts. Contact us to discuss your needs.

What's included in the support?

Support includes bug fixes, updates, and technical assistance. Professional and Enterprise plans include priority support and faster response times.

Are there any hidden fees?

No! Our pricing is transparent and includes everything listed in the plan. Any add-on services are clearly priced separately.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards, bank transfers, and PayPal. We also offer flexible payment plans for larger projects.
